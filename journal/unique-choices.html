Unique Choices

An authorized dealer can only sell what the manufacturer allocates that year. A curated pre-owned boutique isn't bound by that — which is how references that were discontinued years ago, or never officially sold in this market, end up back in circulation.

We actively source discontinued dials, early production runs, and references that collectors specifically search for because they're no longer being made. Some come from private collections, others from trade with other dealers we've worked with for years.

What ends up on our shelves changes often. If there's a specific reference you've been hunting for, it's worth asking us directly — we frequently have pieces in hand before they're ever listed online.
